Autonomous driving and reinforcement learning

3D-First MetaDrive Baseline

A reproducible autonomous-driving study in the MetaDrive simulator. The vehicle learns from LiDAR and navigation state, with standard PPO compared against recurrent PPO with LSTM memory. The repository also includes IDM demonstrations, evaluation on unseen road scenarios, and driving replay tools.

Approach

LiDAR and navigation state · PPO and recurrent PPO · held-out road scenarios

Result

A controlled simulator study of memory and generalization for autonomous driving.

Repository ↗

Top-down replay from the best locally reproduced PPO checkpoint on a held-out scenario.

Autonomous driving and adaptive memory

CarRacing Research Sandbox

A research sandbox for testing adaptive memory and PPO-style agents on changing control problems, including CarRacing. It contains benchmark configurations, recurrent and feed-forward baselines, training plots, evaluation tools, and gameplay-recording utilities.

Approach

Continuous-control PPO · adaptive memory experiments · recorded gameplay

Result

A reusable environment for comparing memory mechanisms under changing visual control conditions.

Repository ↗

Stochastic gameplay from the best locally reproduced continuous-action PPO checkpoint.

Result

Local replays make the learned behavior inspectable across Position-Only CartPole and Pendulum tasks.

Computer vision and robotics

RGB-D Pose Estimation

A classical end-to-end RGB-D baseline for rigid-part detection and 6-DoF pose estimation. It combines colour and depth clustering with geometric pose hypotheses, trimmed ICP refinement, and depth and silhouette verification against a CAD model.

Approach

RGB-D segmentation · CAD geometry · ICP refinement · silhouette verification

Result

A transparent geometric baseline whose predicted masks, axes, and pose checks can be inspected directly.

Repository ↗

End-to-end baseline predictions on the strongest clean held-out validation scene; coloured outlines and axes are model outputs.
